⚡ Privacy Policy

EV Savings  •  Effective Date: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the EV Savings: EV Charge & Range Utility Suite mobile application ("the App"), developed by Yurii Ishchenko ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when using our utility calculators.

1. Offline-First & Zero Personal Data Collection

EV Savings is designed with privacy at its core:

  • No User Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use the App.
  • Local On-Device Processing: All calculations (charging time, electricity tariffs, battery health SOH estimates, solar BESS simulations, and trip logs) are processed entirely offline on your device.
  • No Cloud Vehicle Sync: We do not ask for or store your vehicle telematics credentials (such as Tesla, Kia/Hyundai, or Nissan cloud logins).
  • Local Data Storage: Vehicle profiles and charge history logs are saved exclusively in your device's private local storage. You can clear or export this data at any time.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing development and offer core features for free, the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data in accordance with its privacy practices, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Apple ID for Advertisers (IDFA) or Google Advertising ID (GAID), subject to your explicit consent via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
  • Usage & Diagnostics: Ad impression data, app performance, and crash metrics.
  • IP Address & General Location: Approximate coarse geographic location (city/country level) for ad localization and fraud prevention.

For more details on how Google processes advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. App Tracking Transparency (ATT) & User Consent

On iOS 14.5 and newer, the App presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. You have full freedom to allow or decline personalized ad tracking. If you decline, you will still receive ads, but they will be non-personalized contextual ads.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

  • Right to Erase: Because all your logs and vehicle profiles are stored locally on your device, deleting the App or using the "Clear Data / Restore Defaults" option inside Settings immediately and permanently deletes all stored data.
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell or share personal information for monetary value.
  • European Residents (GDPR): You can adjust your consent choices for personalized ads at any time in the App Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is a general audience automotive utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
