Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

🔌 Does EV Savings require an active internet connection?

No. EV Savings is engineered with a 100% offline-first architecture. All math engines, charging timers, battery health models, and trip calculators work entirely on your device without cellular or Wi-Fi data.

⏱️ How does the Live Charge Tracker calculate charging speed?

The Live Charge Tracker calculates real-time power from your selected voltage and current (Power = Voltage × Current), factoring in charging efficiency and battery capacity. You can easily calibrate the percentage at any moment using the [-5%], [-1%], [+1%], [+5%] buttons to match your vehicle's dashboard.

📄 How can I export my charge history or Battery Health Certificate?

Go to Settings > Data Management to export your full charge history as JSON or CSV. You can also generate and share an official PDF Battery Health Certificate directly from the Battery SOH screen.

🔄 How do I reset or restore app preferences?

In Settings, you will find options to clear your historical charge logs or restore all default vehicle profiles and tariff configurations.
