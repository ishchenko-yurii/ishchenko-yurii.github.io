Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Does EV Savings require an internet connection?

No. EV Savings is built with a 100% offline-first architecture. All calculation engines, battery models, tariff simulators, and local logs operate completely without cellular or Wi-Fi data.

❓ How does the Live Charge Tracker calculate charge speed?

The Live Charge Tracker uses a physical power formula \((P = V \times I)\) combined with battery capacity and efficiency \(\eta\) to calculate added energy over elapsed time \(\Delta t\). You can also fine-tune the SoC percentage with quick calibration buttons to match your car's dashboard.

❓ How can I export my charge logs or Battery Health Certificate?

Navigate to Settings > Data Management to export your charge logs in JSON/CSV format, or generate an official PDF Battery Health Certificate directly from the Battery SOH screen.

❓ How can I reset my app data?

In Settings, you will find options to clear your charge log history or restore all default vehicle profiles and preferences.
